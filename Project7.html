Project Overview

Help the Wizard to train his fireball damage using monster as target practice.

Features

  • Player movement & spellcasting with MageController
  • Enemy patrol, chase, and attack using EnemyController
  • Attack, damage, get-hit, and death mechanics
  • Animation system driven by events
  • Infinite parallax background
  • Sound effects for actions and combat
  • Player and Enemy Health System

Technologies Used

Unity 6, C#
