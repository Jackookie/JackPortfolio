Project Overview

Control your car and finish your lap with the best time record.

Features

  • Player control car in top down view
  • Lap system such as current lap, lap best time.
  • Smoke as particle system
  • Camera follow car
  • Track where the car can only move inside and cannot go outside
  • Sound effects for actions and combat

Technologies Used

Unity 6, C#
